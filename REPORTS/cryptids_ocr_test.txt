THE HOLLOW STITCH NURSERY
 DEPARTMENT OF UNNATURAL FAUNA
RECOVERED ARCHIVE
There are creatures in this world that existed long before the Hollow Stitch Nursery. The Nursery
did not create the things in these records - at least, not most of them. Before its halls became
associated with stitched toys, abandoned experiments, and things that learned to breathe without
lungs, it maintained another purpose: collection. Reports arrived from forests, mountains, swamps,
rivers, deserts, and forgotten roads. Investigators were dispatched. Witnesses were interviewed.
Tracks were measured. Samples were collected. When possible, specimens were brought home.
HOW TO READ THE FILES
Each cryptid entry preserves the traditional legend first, followed by the surviving Hollow Stitch
record. Nursery classifications describe only what its investigators claimed to observe. OBSERVED
means staff reported direct contact. TRACKED means an active investigation was maintained.
RECOVERED means physical evidence was obtained. CONFIRMED means the archive contains
evidence the Nursery considered sufficient. CONTAINED is the classification no archivist wanted
to see beside the word escaped.
ARCHIVIST NOTICE
Traditional accounts have been preserved as inspiration whenever possible, but legends vary across
communities and generations. The Hollow Stitch material is fictional and should not be mistaken
for a claim about the cultures or traditions behind these creatures. One instruction appears
repeatedly throughout the recovered field manuals: Do not assume folklore is inaccurate simply
because it is impossible. When generations of people agree that a place, sound, road, or stretch of
water should be left alone, the Nursery's safest recommendation was usually the simplest one: leave
it alone.
Thirty files were recovered. The original inventory lists more.
