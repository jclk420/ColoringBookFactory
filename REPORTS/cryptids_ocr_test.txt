DEPARTMENT OF UNNATURAL FAUNA
RECOVERED ARCHIVE
There are creatures in this world that existed long before the Hollow Stitch Nursery. The Nursery
did not create the things in these records - at least, not most of them. Before its halls became
associated with stitched toys, abandoned experiments, and things that learned to breathe without
lungs, it maintained another purpose: collection. Reports arrived from forests, mountains, swamps,
rivers, deserts, and forgotten roads. Investigators were dispatched. Witnesses were interviewed.
Tracks were measured. Samples were collected. When possible, specimens were brought home.
HOW TO READ THE FILES
Each cryptid entry preserves the traditional legend first, followed by the surviving Hollow Stitch
record. Nursery classifications describe only what its investigators claimed to observe. OBSERVED
means staff reported direct contact. TRACKED means an active investigation was maintained.
RECOVERED means physical evidence was obtained. CONFIRMED means the archive contains
evidence the Nursery considered sufficient. CONTAINED is the classification no archivist wanted
to see beside the word escaped.
ARCHIVIST NOTICE
Traditional accounts have been preserved as inspiration whenever possible, but legends vary across
communities and generations. The Hollow Stitch material is fictional and should not be mistaken
for a claim about the cultures or traditions behind these creatures. One instruction appears
repeatedly throughout the recovered field manuals: Do not assume folklore is inaccurate simply
because it is impossible. When generations of people agree that a place, sound, road, or stretch of
water should be left alone, the Nursery's safest recommendation was usually the simplest one: leave
it alone.
Thirty files were recovered. The original inventory lists more.

CLASSIFICATION Cursed Revenant / Death Herald

LEGEND
Across the plains of Venezuela and Colombia, travelers tell of El Silbon, an impossibly thin wanderer
carrying a sack of bones. His whistle is the warning and the trap. When it sounds close, he may still be far
away. When it sounds faint, tradition warns that he may already be near. Stories describe a cursed spirit
doomed to roam the night, his bones knocking together as he walks.
HOLLOW STITCH RECORD
The Nursery's first file began with an unmarked package containing seven photographs. Six showed empty
roads. The seventh showed a thin figure with a sack. Three nights later, staff heard a whistle beyond the
western fence. At sunrise, a human finger bone rested against the door. Seven bones appeared over the
following weeks. Years later, investigator Thomas Reed vanished while tracking the Whistler. An eighth
bone arrived six months afterward with Reed's identification tag tied around it. Someone had scratched one
number beneath his name: 8.
NURSERY DIRECTIVE
Do not investigate the whistle. Do not whistle back. Count everyone inside. If the second count does not
match the first, do not count again.
END FILE HSN-CR-001

The Winged Ape of the Cascades
HSN. FILE HSN-CR-004
CLASSIFICATION Aerial Hominid / Mountain Cryptid
THREAT LEVEL IV - SEVERE
ORIGIN Pacific Northwest, United States
DOCUMENTED ABILITY Flight; exceptional strength; rapid vertical movement
OBSERVED WEAKNESS
Unknown
STATUS OBSERVED - Active reports
LEGEND
Batsquatch stories describe a large, ape-like creature with leathery wings, glowing or unusual eyes, and a
powerful build. Reports are most closely associated with the forests and mountains of the Pacific Northwest,
where witnesses have described a shape too large for a bat and too airborne for anything resembling a
primate.
HOLLOW STITCH RECORD
Hollow Stitch observers placed bait near a high ridge after repeated night sightings. Nothing touched it for
six days. On the seventh morning the bait cage was found crushed flat from above, though there were no
tracks around it. A strip of dark membrane clung to the steel. During transport, the sample began twitching
whenever the vehicle passed beneath power lines. It vanished from the evidence locker that night. The
ceiling vent above the locker was still screwed shut from the inside.
NURSERY DIRECTIVE
Do not assume the tree line is the boundary of the search. Check above it.
END FILE HSN-CR-004

The Serpent of Crescent Lake
HSN. FILE HSN-CR-007
CLASSIFICATION Freshwater Serpent / Lake Cryptid
THREAT LEVEL Il - MODERATE
ORIGIN Newfoundland, Canada
DOCUMENTED ABILITY Long-duration submersion; sudden surface movement
OBSERVED WEAKNESS
Unknown
STATUS OBSERVED - Nonaggressive
LEGEND
Cressie is the name given to a long-bodied lake creature reported from Crescent Lake in Newfoundland.
Descriptions vary, but many accounts involve a serpentine animal moving just below the surface or leaving
an unusual wake. Unlike more openly violent water legends, Cressie is usually described as elusive rather
than predatory.
HOLLOW STITCH RECORD
Nursery observers recorded an elongated wake crossing a calm inlet without any visible animal at its head. A
floating camera was deployed the next morning. It returned to shore on its own three hours later, lens
cracked and memory card missing. The camera body contained lake water on the outside and dry sand
inside the sealed battery compartment. No one could explain the sand. Crescent Lake has no shoreline
matching the mineral sample.
NURSERY DIRECTIVE
Observation only. Do not pursue submerged movement into narrow water.
END FILE HSN-CR-007

The Roadside Watcher
HSN. FILE HSN-CR-008
CLASSIFICATION Unclassified Humanoid / Nocturnal Entity
THREAT LEVEL Il- HIGH
ORIGIN Dover, Massachusetts, United States
DOCUMENTED ABILITY a. .
Extreme agility; low-light movement
OBSERVED WEAKNESS .
Avoids prolonged exposure and groups
STATUS CONFIRMED SIGHTING - No capture
LEGEND
The Dover Demon legend began with a cluster of reported encounters near Dover, Massachusetts, in 1977.
Witnesses described a small, thin humanoid with a large smooth head, long fingers, and glowing or
reflective eyes. It was seen near roads, walls, and wooded areas, appearing only briefly before disappearing
into the dark.
HOLLOW STITCH RECORD
A Hollow Stitch investigator reconstructed the original route and placed trail cameras along a stone wall. At
2:17 A.M., every camera captured the same figure at the same instant, despite being spaced nearly half a mile
apart. In each image the creature faced the camera. In the final frame it was gone, replaced by a close
photograph of the investigator asleep in his motel room. The motel door had remained locked all night.
NURSERY DIRECTIVE
If it stops moving when observed, do not approach. It may be waiting for you to look away.

The Spade-Headed Visitor
HSN. FILE HSN-CR-009
CLASSIFICATION Extraterrestrial / Atmospheric Entity
THREAT LEVEL IV - SEVERE
ORIGIN Flatwoods, West Virginia, United States
DOCUMENTED ABILITY Gliding movement; chemical or noxious emission
OBSERVED WEAKNESS
Unknown
STATUS RECORDED - Origin unresolved
LEGEND
The Flatwoods Monster entered American folklore after a 1952 encounter in West Virginia. Witnesses
described a tall figure with a dark body, glowing eyes, and a head or hood shaped like a spade or ace. The
encounter was also associated with a strange odor that reportedly caused nausea and irritation.
HOLLOW STITCH RECORD
Nursery chemists obtained a soil sample from the reported encounter area. When warmed, the sample
released a sharp metallic odor and caused every compass in the laboratory to point toward the Nursery
basement instead of north. The effect lasted eleven minutes. During those eleven minutes, a sealed
basement door opened by itself. Security found no intruder - only a triangular burn mark on the floor anda
smell matching the sample.
NURSERY DIRECTIVE
If the air smells metallic and your compass turns toward the building, evacuate the lower level.
END FILE HSN-CR-009

The Boggy Creek Beast
HSN. FILE HSN-CR-010
CLASSIFICATION Hominid Cryptid / Swamp Stalker
THREAT LEVEL Il- HIGH
ORIGIN â€˜Arkansas, United States
DOCUMENTED ABILITY Exceptional strength; silent movement through swamp terrain
OBSERVED WEAKNESS . . -
Avoids sustained pursuit

LEGEND
The Fouke Monster, also known as the Boggy Creek Monster, is associated with reports near Fouke,
Arkansas. Witnesses have described a large, hairy, ape-like creature moving through swamp and woodland.
Tracks, strange calls, and close encounters helped make the creature one of the best-known regional
cryptids in the United States.
HOLLOW STITCH RECORD
Nursery trackers followed a line of three-toed impressions through knee-deep mud. The tracks ended at a
cypress tree with no prints leaving the area. Thirty feet above them, the bark had been stripped away in long
parallel gouges. A hair sample recovered from the trunk contained keratin but no usable cellular material.
Under magnification, every strand appeared hollow. One strand produced a low knocking sound when
sealed inside a glass vial.
NURSERY DIRECTIVE
If the tracks stop without turning, check the trees before checking behind you.
